## Overview

SkripsiMate is an AI-powered assistant designed to solve a major hurdle in higher education: the initial research block. Many students struggle to identify a thesis topic that is both academically viable and personally engaging.

This tool utilizes a Context-Aware Prompting system. Instead of generating generic topics, SkripsiMate guides users through a series of specific inquiries—department, current academic year, geographic location, hobbies, and career aspirations—to produce research titles that are relevant, tailored, and meaningful to the individual student.

## Key Features

* **Personalized Brainstorming:** Synthesizes academic requirements with personal interests and future goals.
* **Contextual Logic:** Factors in location and study level to ensure the generated ideas are feasible.
* **Modern Interface:** Designed with a clean, high-fidelity UI to provide a professional and seamless user experience.
* **Guided Discovery:** Uses a step-by-step approach to help students refine their thoughts into concrete research directions.
